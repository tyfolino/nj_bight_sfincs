## The short version

**Waves help v4, about as much as they help v3.** Turning waves on improves the high-water-mark fit on run B by ΔRMSE −0.069 m (95% CI −0.098 to −0.047; 110 marks, median estimator, 50 m radius), against −0.064 on v3. Every basin group gains, the NY bays and the NJ back bays most. Run B's walls and inflow moves change nothing measurable against run A (−0.003 m, CI −0.013 to +0.009). v4 still trails v3 by about +0.19 m on the 94 shared marks, but that gap is mostly a handful of marks: against the v3 re-run (`v3fix_on`), 8 of 94 marks carry ~92% of the squared-error difference. Five are rain-pond marks beside coastal lakes (6130 by Wreck Pond, 6121, 6122, 6124, 6129; ~75%) that v3 fills with rain it never lets soak in (no infiltration; v4 uses CN), so v3 is right there for the wrong reason (FINDINGS part 4 (d)). One is the Keansburg pocket mark 6155 (~13%; run B had no levee weir, restored in `v4_refreeze`). The other two are 6066 (Great Bay, ~3%) and 6160 (Raritan Bay, ~2%). So most of the gap is a rain/infiltration difference plus a fixed levee, not a basin-wide surge deficit; the real Raritan Bay low remains (−0.4 to −0.7 m at 6098, 6159, 6160).

## Caveats

- **Keansburg pocket marks 6133 / 6155 / 6156:** runs A and B had NO levee weir (FINDINGS §38; fixed in `v4_refreeze`), so these read high (6155 +1.74 m in run B). Discount them in every A/B number; the refreeze arms are the real read.
- **7 crest-hole gauges:** their observed records stop 10-29 03:54 and restart 10-30 04:00, the whole crest (FINDINGS §60f). Their "observed peak" here is a post-crest sample, so a large positive error there (Inside Thorofare +0.54) is an artefact. They still lack `record_ends` (STATUS item 3).
- **NY-bay waves-on crests ±0.1–0.15 m:** hourly SnapWave updates amplify the harbour seiche; the Sandy Hook / Narrows / South Amboy / Keansburg lifts are partly phase (swing test 62328665 queued).
- **v4 vs v3 is not like-for-like on rain:** v4 infiltrates (CN), v3 does not, so the rain-pond marks (6130, 6121, 6122, 6124, 6129) favour v3.
- **v3 and v4 score different MOTF rasters;** CSI is not comparable across domains, and waves-off CSI is not ranked against waves-on.
- **Gauges on high faces:** Absecon Creek and Inside Thorofare only see the crest (in v3 too).
- **Open in the refreeze list:** the Raritan / Newark Bay low (−0.4 to −0.7 m; the harbour channels fixed Newark Bay but not Raritan), the Schuylkill backing up below Fairmount Dam, the Christina inflow ponding, Little Egg Harbor (pre-Sandy charts unchecked), and the unresolved bed candidates at Cape May Point, the C&D Canal and Lewes.
- **These are runs A and B, not the refreeze.** `v4_refreeze` adds the weirs, the corner-convention meteo fix and the new bed, so every number here gets re-baselined, not delta-ed.
